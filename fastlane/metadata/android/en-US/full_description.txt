Your phone stops ringing when it shouldn't.

That's the entire product. Orange runs silently in the background, screens
incoming calls, and blocks the unwanted ones before the ring ever reaches
you. No menus to learn. No settings to configure. No account to create.

Orange catches international scam corridors, Caribbean premium-rate traps,
Wangiri callback scams (both classic one-ring and the newer recorded-message
variant), structurally impossible phone numbers, withheld caller IDs, and
carrier-flagged spoofed calls — all without reading your contacts or
connecting to any server.

If someone calls pretending to be from your local police headquarters,
Orange recognizes the number and shows a warning with a one-tap "Call my
family" button. If you try to call back a number Orange just blocked,
it warns you before you connect.

Emergency numbers — 911, 112, 110, 119 — always ring through.

Free. No ads. No subscription. Not now, not later.
